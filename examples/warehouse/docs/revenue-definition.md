# What "revenue" means here

Revenue is recognised at fulfilment, not at order placement. An order placed on the 31st and
shipped on the 1st belongs to the following month.

Finance and the growth team both use the word and do not mean the same thing by it: growth
counts bookings at placement, because that is the number their campaigns move. Neither is
wrong, and the reporting layer carries both under named perspectives rather than picking one.

Refunds are netted in the period of the refund, not the period of the original sale.
